# Lets learn basic Sanskrit words.
Note: Sanskrit always follows:
Singular → Dual → Plural
This is the biggest difference from Telugu/English — and once you get it, everything becomes systematic.

<br>

## Basic:
|Gender|Singular|Dual|Plural|
|---------|------|------|------|
|I/We | अहम् | आवाम् | वयम् |
| You | त्वम् | युवाम् | यूयम् |
|Masculine - He (near)| एषः | एतौ | एते |
|Masculine - He (far)| सः | तौ | ते |
|Feminine - She (near) |एषा | एते | एताः |
|Feminine - She (far) | सा | ते | ताः |
| Neuter - It (near) | एतत् | एते | एतानि |
|Neuter - It (far) | तत् | ते | तानि |

<br>

## First Person (I / We)
|English|Sanskrit|Usage|
| ----|---------|---------------------------------------------------|
| I   |अहम्     |अहं ग्रामं गच्छामि → I go to the village                | 
| We (2)      |आवाम् | आवां ग्रामं गच्छावः → We two go to the village    |
| We (many)   |वयम् | वयं ग्रामं गच्छामः → We all go to the village      |

<br>

## Second Person (You)
|English|Sanskrit|Usage|
| --------------|---------|------------------------------------------|
| You (singular)|त्वम्    | त्वं ग्रामं गच्छसि → You go to the village        |
| You (respect male)|भवान् | भवान् ग्रामं गच्छति → You (respect) go to the village |
| You (respect female)|भवति | भवति ग्रामं गच्छति → You (respect) go to the village |
| You (2)       |युवाम्   | युवां ग्रामं गच्छथः → You two go                  |
| You (many)    |यूयम्    | यूयं ग्रामं गच्छथ → You all go                  |

<br>

## Third Person (Masculine - He / They)
|English|Sanskrit|Usage|
| --------------|---------|------------------------------------------|
|He (singular & near)| एषः| एषः धावति → This man runs|
|| | एषः पुरुषः धावति → This man runs|
|He (singular & far)| सः| सः धावति → That man runs|
|They (2 & near)| एतौ| एतौ धावतः → These two run|
|They (2 & far)| तौ| तौ धावतः → Those two run|
|They (many & near)| एते| एते धावन्ति → These (many) run|
|They (many & far)| ते| ते धावन्ति → Those (many) run|

<br>

## Third Person (Feminine - He / They)
|English|Sanskrit|Usage|
| --------------|---------|------------------------------------------|
|She (singular & near)| एष| एषा गच्छति → This woman goes|
|She (singular & far)| सा| सा गच्छति → That woman goes|
||| सा नारी गच्छति → That woman goes|
|They (2 & near)| एते| एते गच्छतः → These two women go|
|They (2 & far)| ते| ते गच्छतः → Those two go|
|They (many & near)| एताः| एताः गच्छन्ति → These women go|
|They (many & far)| ताः| ताः गच्छन्ति → Those women go|

<br>

## Neuter (Things / Animals)
|English|Sanskrit|Usage|
| --------------|---------|------------------------------------------|
|It/This (singular & near)| एतत् / इदम् | एतत् पतति → This falls|
||| Note: इदम् is base form. एतत् is commonly used in sentences|
|It (singular & far)| तत् | तत् पतति → That falls|
|They (2 & near)| एते| एते पततः → These two fall|
|Those (2 & far)| ते| ते पततः → Those two fall|
|They (many & near)| एतानि| एतानि पतन्ति → These fall|
|They (many & near)| एतानि| एतानि फलानि पतन्ति → These fruits fall|
|They (many & far)| तानि| तानि पतन्ति → Those fall|
|They (many & far)| तानि| तानि वृक्षाणि तिष्ठन्ति → Those trees stand|

<br>

## Basic Pattern
- ति → singular
- तः → dual
- न्ति → plural

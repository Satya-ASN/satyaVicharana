# अव्यय - Indeclinables

## अव्यय = a word that does NOT change its form
- No gender change
- No singular/dual/plural change
- No case endings
## Categories
- Question words
- Connecting words
- Place/time words

## 1. अद्य (today)
|Usage in Sanskrit | Usage in English |
|---------|---------------------------------------------------|
| अहं अद्य पठामि | I study today |
|सः अद्य गच्छति | He goes today |
|वयं अद्य मिलामः | We meet today |
|सा अद्य लिखति | She writes today |
|ते अद्य आगच्छन्ति | They come today |

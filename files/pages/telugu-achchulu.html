Telugu Vowels (అచ్చులు)

📚 Script Basics ⏱️ 15 min

Telugu has 16 vowels called అచ్చులు (Acchulu). These are the foundation of the Telugu script, and every consonant carries an inherent 'a' (అ) sound.

అచ్చులు

అ a as u in 'bUt'
ఆ ā as a in 'fAther
ఇ i as e in 'Inside'
ఈ ī as e in 'Easy'
ఉ u as u in 'sUper'
ఊ ū as oo in 'balOOn'
ఋ aRu as r in tRue'
ౠ aRoo as OO in 'marOOn'
ఎ e as E in 'mAjor'
ఏ ē as a in 'mAin'
ఐ ai as i in 'maIn'
ఒ o as o in 'Open'
ఓ ō as o in 'hOpe'
ఔ au as ou in 'OUt'
అం aṃ (anusvara) as um in 'sUM'
అః aḥ (visarga)

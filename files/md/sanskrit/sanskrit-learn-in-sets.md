# Learn Sanskrit in sets

## Perhaps – Certainly
|words|Sasnkrit Usage|English Meaning|
| ----|---------|---------------------------------------------------|
|Perhaps → कदाचित् | कदाचित् सः आगच्छति | Perhaps he comes |
|Certainly → नूनम् / निश्चितम् | नूनं सः आगच्छति | Certainly he comes |

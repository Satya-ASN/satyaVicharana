<h2>Svarajathi 2 (Samba Shiva) </h2>

<h3>
rāgam khamās (mēḻakarta 28, harikāmbhōji janyarāga)
<br>
ārōhaṇa: S . . . . M1, G3 M1 . P . D2 N2 . Ṡ
<br>
avarōhaṇa: Ṡ . N2 D2 . P . M1 G3 . R2 . S
<br>
tāḻam: chatusra jāti tripuṭa tāḻam (ādi)
<br>
rūpakarta: శ్రీ chinnakṛṣṇa dāsar
<br>
<br>
pallavi
</h3>
sāmbaśivāyanavē rājitagiri
śāmbhavīmanōharā parātparā kṛpākarā śrī

charaṇaṃ 1
nīvē guru daivambani yē vēḻanu sēvimpuchu sadā madini śiva

charaṇaṃ 2
parama dayānidhi vanuchu maruvaka nā hṛdayamuna
mahādēva mahāprabhō sundara nayana suravara dāyaka bhavabhaya haraśiva

charaṇaṃ 3
sthira madhurāpuramuna varamulosagu haruni niratamunu dalachi

charaṇaṃ 4
śrī śubhakara śaśi makuṭadhara jaya vijaya tripuraharā-
śritajana lōlādbhuta guṇaśīlā kṛtanutapālā patituni lōlā-
mudambala raṅga padābjamulandu padambulujērchu paśupatini
jñānamu dhyānamu snānamu pānamu
dānamu mānamu abhimānamanuchu
kanikaramuna charaṇambulu kanu-
gonu śṛtulannutula śaraṇanuchu (sāmba)

charaṇaṃ 5 (khaṇḍa gati - 5 kāla)
sāresāregu nī nāma mantraṃ kōrinānu nī pādāmbuja mantram
dāsuḍau chinni kṛṣṇuniki dikku nīvēyani śokkanāthuni nammukoni

svarāḥ

<h2>Muddugare Yasoda<h2>
Annamacharya Kirthana
Raga: Kurinji
Tala: Adi
Lyrics with meaning

muddugArE yaSOda muMgiTi mutyamu vIDu 
This sweet boy is like a pearl on the threshold of yasoda
tiddarAni mahimala dEvakI sutuDu 
This indescribable magician is the son of Devaki

Charanam1
aMta niMta golletala aracEti mANikyamu 
He is like a ruby on the top of the palms of small and big cowherds
paMtamADE kaMsunipAli vajramu 
He is srong and invincible like a diamond for stubborn Kamsa
kAMtula mUDu lOkAla garuDa paccapUsa 
In brightness he is like a big emerald in the three worlds
ceMtala mAlOnunna cinnikRuShNuDu ||
But he is the small Krishna who is very close to us and one among us
Charanam2
ratikELi rukmiNiki raMgumOvi pagaDamu 
He is like coral on the olured lips of Rukmini
miti gOvardhanapu gOmEdhikamu |
He is like a zircon of Govardhana.
satamai SaMKa cakrAla saMdula vaiDUryamu 
He is the cat's eye embossed in the crevices of sankha and chakra
gatiyai mammugAcE kamalAkShuDu
He is our lotus eyed lord and saviour
Charanam3
kALiMguni talalapaina puShyarAgamu 
He is like a topaz on the head of Kaalinga
ElETi SrI vEMkaTAdri yiMdra nIlamu 
He is the rular of Venkatadri like a blue saphire
pAlajala nidhilOna pAyani divyaratnamu 
In the oceon of milk he is unattainable divine gem
bAluni vale tirigI padmanABuDu
He who goes about like a small boy in fact Padmanabha
